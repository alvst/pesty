Common questions

How do I paste a clip?

Open Pesty-Alvie with its default ⌃⌘V shortcut. Use the arrow keys to pick a clip and press Return, or press ⌘1–⌘9 to grab one of the first nine clips.

Direct paste requires a fresh Accessibility grant for Pesty-Alvie because it intentionally does not reuse upstream Pesty's signed identity.

Pesty-Alvie is not saving what I copy
  • Make sure Pesty-Alvie is running — look for its icon in the menu bar. Open it with ⌃⌘V.
  • Ignore passwords is on by default, so clips marked concealed by password managers are intentionally skipped.
  • Check the History limit in Settings if older clips are dropping off.
How do I change the keyboard shortcut?

Open Settings (menu bar icon → Settings, or ⌘,) and click the shortcut field next to Show Pesty-Alvie, then press the key combination you want.

Is my clipboard private?

Clipboard history stays in Pesty-Alvie's separate local or iCloud Drive folder and is not sent to the developer. Inline link previews can contact the copied website for metadata. See the privacy notes for details.

How does iCloud sync work?

Sync is optional and off by default. When enabled in Settings, your history and pinboards sync between your own Macs through your iCloud Drive. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is enabled in System Settings. The developer never has access to this data.

How do I clear my history or reset Pesty-Alvie?

Open Settings → Data → Clear Clipboard History. This permanently removes stored clips from your Mac.

How do I uninstall Pesty-Alvie?
  • Quit Pesty-Alvie and move Pesty-Alvie.app from Applications to the Trash.
  • Its separate history remains in ~/Library/Application Support/Pesty-Alvie until you remove it intentionally.
  • Upstream Pesty and its data are unaffected.
What are the system requirements?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal — runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
