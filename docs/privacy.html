Pesty Privacy Notes

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Pesty is an independently maintained fork of the open-source Pesty clipboard manager for macOS.

What Pesty stores

Pesty keeps a history of what you copy so you can paste it again. This history — text, links, images, files, and colors — is stored in its separate ~/Library/Application Support/Pesty folder (or its app sandbox container), with restrictive file permissions.

What Pesty does not do

  • Pesty does not sell your data or send clipboard history to the developer.
  • Pesty has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, or ads.
  • Clips marked as concealed by password managers are ignored and never stored.

Network behavior

Clipboard data is not uploaded to a Pesty server. Inline previews for links can request page metadata, images, and icons from the copied website. Avoid inline link previews for sensitive URLs. Optional iCloud sync uses Apple's services as described below.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, your clipboard history is stored in your own iCloud Drive/Pesty folder so it can sync between your Macs through Apple's iCloud. This data stays within your personal Apple account; the developer does not receive it. Sync is off by default.

Permissions

The local Pesty build can optionally use Accessibility permission to paste the selected clip into the app you were using. Its separate signed identity, com.alvst.pesty, creates a separate System Settings entry and requires a fresh grant.

Contact & Support

Questions or need help? Visit the support page or open an issue in the Pesty fork. Upstream Pesty remains available at momenbasel/pesty.
